Market Analysis
Ranks Volatility and Jump indices live per contract type. The "Confidence" column is a z-score-based confidence (0–100), not a raw percentage — it measures how statistically unusual the observed skew is against the expected random baseline, given the sample size, so a 60% skew on 20 ticks isn't treated the same as a 60% skew on 100 ticks. Still a heuristic, not a prediction — Deriv's synthetic indices are designed to be close to statistically fair.
